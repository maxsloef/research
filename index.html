I'm Max. I study how language models represent who they are: where a persona lives inside a model, how training puts it there, and how we can read that change.

X / Twitter GitHub Hugging Face

Now

I'm on the MATS founder track with Nick Fitz. Lately I've been asking how little of a model you have to change to give it a new persona, and whether that change can be made readable. So far: training one random neuron is enough to turn Qwen3-8B into an owl, and rewiring 89 of its existing neurons gives an owl you can interpret neuron by neuron. Both are written up below.

Before MATS, I was early at Goodfire (research below), websim, and Flora, and before that at SPC, Uniswap, and Snap.

  • One random neuron makes an owl October 29, 2026
